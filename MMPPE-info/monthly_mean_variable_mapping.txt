================================================================================
MMPPE MONTHLY MEAN OUTPUT -- MAPPING TO CESM/CAM6 (MG2/MAM4) FIELDS
================================================================================
Source protocol: MMPPE-info/MMPPE.md, "Model Output Variables" -> "Monthly
Mean". Companion to 6hourly_variable_mapping.txt -- read that file's header
first for the match-key legend and general caveats (MasterFieldList.txt has
no COSP/MODIS fields, etc.); not repeated in full here.

MMPPE.md says the monthly-mean stream should carry "All 6h outputs ... except
for cosp" PLUS the additional variables listed below. So:

  - Every variable in 6hourly_variable_mapping.txt SECTION 1 (3D) and
    SECTION 2 (2D main table) applies here too, at monthly-mean frequency
    instead of 6-hourly instantaneous. Not re-listed below except where the
    SAME NAME reappears in the monthly table with a DIFFERENT MEANING (see
    "NAME COLLISION" notes) or a materially different match.
  - SECTION 3 (the "(optional)" MODIS table) is explicitly EXCLUDED from
    monthly mean per MMPPE.md's own remark ("except for cosp").
  - Everything below is either new to the monthly list, or flagged because
    it collides with a 6-hourly name but means something different here.

Match:            NONE-BEST

MMPPE Variable:   ANG_550nm_865nm
Description:      Angstroem parameter 550nm-865nm
Unit:             1
CESM Field:       ANGSTRM_550_865
Match:            CLOSE
Notes:            A new field, ANGSTRM_550_865, was added to
                  src/physics/rrtmg/radiation.F90 (see
                  ppe_changes_incorporated.txt section 7e), alongside (not
                  replacing) the earlier 400-700nm ANGSTRM field (ported
                  as-is from MMPPE-info/radiation.F90's E3SM formula, see
                  7c). ANGSTRM_550_865 uses RRTMG SW band 10 (441-625nm,
                  idx_sw_diag -- the same band AODVIS uses) for "550nm" and
                  band 8 (778-1242nm, idx_sw_diag-2) for "865nm", chosen
                  over band 9 (625-778nm, idx_sw_diag-1) because 865nm
                  falls outside band 9's range but inside band 8's, and
                  band 8's log-wavelength center (~983nm) is roughly twice
                  as close to 865nm in log-space as band 9's (~697nm) --
                  see the radiation_tend code comments for the full
                  derivation. Not a bit-exact 550.0/865.0nm pair (RRTMG has
                  no band boundary there), but a real, purpose-chosen
                  native field rather than a ported or offline-derived
                  approximation. Day-only and not on by default; add via
                  fincl. The 400-700nm ANGSTRM and the AODVIS(550)/
                  AODNIR(1020)-derived fallback recipe both remain
                  available too. Not yet validated against real model
                  output.

================================================================================
SUMMARY
================================================================================
New variables introduced by the monthly-mean table (i.e. not already covered
by "all 6h outputs"): ~64. Of those:
  - ~26 EXACT or CLOSE (mostly the standard dynamics/thermodynamics fields:
    zg, hur, hus, cl, cli, clw, wap, ta, pfull, CDNC, ICNC, ps, psl, ptp, ts,
    tas, burden_dms, burden_so2, lsm, CCN_0.300, and the per-species
    TAU_COMP_* fields)
  - ~10 DERIVED (mode-summed mixing ratios/emission fluxes: SO4, BC, OC, DU,
    SS, WAT, emi_so4, emi_du, emi_ss, and unit conversions for DMS/SO2/
    precip components)
  - ~1 APPROX + DERIVED (CT_TIME)
  - ~13 NONE-BEST, most notably:
      * emi_bb_so2/emi_bb_bc/emi_bb_oc -- no native field separates
        biomass-burning emissions from the combined total, which is
        directly relevant to this branch's srf_emis_scale_*_bb PPE knobs
        (see notes under emi_bb_so2 above and ppe_changes_incorporated.txt
        section 6b).
      * dzhalf, rho, dew2, albedo -- standard derivable diagnostics with no
        native field.
      * ABS_COMP_OC/SO4/DU/SS/WAT_550nm, TAU_COMP_WAT_550nm, and all 6
        ABS_2D_MODE_* -- CAM's per-species AOD/absorption decomposition
        doesn't go as far as the protocol wants (absorption is only broken
        out for BC; no species gets a water-specific AOD term).

Two collisions worth flagging explicitly: this table's "SO4" and "BC" reuse
the exact same names as 6-hourly's surface-mass-concentration variables but
mean 3D mass MIXING RATIOS here -- don't conflate the two when building an
actual output-variable list/script.
